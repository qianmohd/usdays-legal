This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the UsDays mobile application on Android and iOS. English is the primary language of this document; a Chinese summary follows.

## 1. Overview

UsDays is designed as a **local-first** couple companion app for anniversaries, diaries, moments, albums, reminders, themes, and related personal journaling features. In the default offline-first mode:

- Core journal content you create stays on your device.
- We do **not** require an account to use the main features.
- We do **not** operate a general-purpose cloud that automatically uploads your diaries, photos, or privacy-lock secrets.

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Information stored on your device (you control)

Depending on how you use the app, UsDays may store locally:

- Couple profile details you enter (e.g., display names, start date, avatars)
- Anniversaries, diaries, moments, wish lists, love-score logs, and similar content
- Photos or images you choose to add
- App preferences (theme, language, reminder settings)
- Optional on-device privacy lock (PIN / pattern) and recovery questions — kept locally
- Local backups you export, and content you import from a backup file

### 2.2 Device and permission-related data

With your permission (or as required by the OS), UsDays may access:

- **Photo library / storage** — only to let you pick, save, or export images and share cards
- **Notifications** — to schedule local anniversary reminders
- **Camera** (if enabled on your build) — only when you choose to capture media inside a supported flow

These permissions are used for the stated feature. We do not use them to build advertising profiles.

### 2.3 What we do not intentionally collect

In the current local-first product mode, we do **not** intentionally collect or receive:

- Your diary text, anniversary notes, or album photos on our servers
- Precise continuous location for tracking
- Contacts lists for marketing
- Advertising IDs for third-party ad networks (UsDays does not include a third-party ads SDK in the described product mode)

If a future feature introduces optional cloud sync, account login, analytics, or crash reporting, we will update this Policy and, where required, ask for additional consent before those features process personal data.

### 2.4 App stores and operating systems

Google Play, Apple App Store, and your device OS may process purchase receipts, device identifiers, crash diagnostics, and install analytics under **their** privacy policies. We may receive limited non-content signals needed to validate purchases or diagnose store-related issues (for example, entitlement status for Premium), but not your private journal content.

## 3. How We Use Information

Local data is used only to provide UsDays features you request: display memories, reminders, themes, privacy lock, backup/restore, Premium entitlements, and related UI.

Contact emails you send to us (e.g., support) are used to respond to your request and improve the product.

## 4. Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal journal content. We do not share on-device diary/photo content with advertisers.

Limited sharing may occur only when:

- You choose to share a card/image through the system share sheet
- Required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights and safety
- With processors who help us operate the business (e.g., email), under confidentiality obligations, without receiving your local journal database

## 5. Retention and Deletion

- Local content remains until you delete it, clear app data, uninstall the app, or overwrite it via import.
- Uninstalling typically removes app-sandbox data; exported backup files you saved elsewhere remain under your control.
- Support emails we receive are retained as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed.

## 6. Security

We design UsDays to keep sensitive content on-device. The optional privacy lock helps prevent casual access on your phone; it is not a substitute for full-disk encryption or physical device security. Please protect your device passcode and any exported backups.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Please use strong device security and store backups carefully.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

UsDays is intended for adults and general audiences. It is **not directed to children under 13** (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided information to us via email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. International Users

The operator is established in the People’s Republic of China. If you use UsDays from another country, your on-device data remains on your device; support communications may be processed where we or our email provider operate. Where applicable law grants you rights of access, correction, deletion, or objection regarding personal data we actually hold (e.g., support emails), contact us at the email below.

## 9. Your Choices

- Revoke photo/notification permissions in system settings
- Delete content inside the app or clear app storage
- Export or delete local backups you created
- Disable privacy lock after verifying your credential
- Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence we hold

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change, and material changes will be reflected in-app and/or at this URL. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
